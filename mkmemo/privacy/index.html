MKmemo privacy policy

Effective 23 September 2026. Operator: Mohamed Kadri, Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

MKmemo is a private tool used by one person, its operator, for his own work. This policy explains what information it reads from Google and Microsoft accounts, why, where it is kept, and how to have it removed.

1. Who is responsible

Mohamed Kadri operates MKmemo on a server he controls. He is the only user. Questions go to engmohamedkadrialy@gmail.com.

2. What MKmemo reads, and why

  • Calendar events (read-only). From the operator's own Google Calendar and Microsoft calendar: the event title, start and end time, and the joining link for a video meeting. Purpose: to send the meeting notetaker to that meeting at the right time. Nothing is written to the calendar.
  • Mail (read-only), on the notetaker's own mailbox only. The notetaker has its own Gmail and Microsoft addresses. MKmemo reads recent messages in those mailboxes to find meeting invitations sent to it. From each message it keeps only the subject line and the meeting link. It does not read the operator's personal mailbox or anyone else's.
  • Meeting audio. When the notetaker is admitted to a meeting, it records the call's audio, transcribes it, and produces a brief for the operator. The audio is deleted after transcription, at the latest within 24 hours. The notetaker is visible in the participant list under its own name; it never joins silently.

3. Google API Services: Limited Use

MKmemo's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, Google user data is used only to schedule and run the notetaker as described above. It is not transferred to others, not used for advertising, not used to train or improve AI or machine-learning models, and not read by any human except the operator himself, and then only for the purpose above.

4. Where the data is kept

All data is stored on a server operated by Mohamed Kadri, in an encrypted database. Access tokens are stored on the same server with restricted file permissions and are used only to perform the reads above. Transcripts are sent to a third-party speech-to-text service for transcription and are not retained by that service under its API terms; no calendar or mail content is sent to any AI service.

5. Sharing

Nothing is sold, rented or shared with third parties. There are no advertisers, no analytics vendors and no data brokers.

6. Retention and deletion

Meeting audio is deleted after transcription. Transcripts and briefs are kept for the operator's own reference. Calendar and mail data is not stored beyond the meeting plan (title, time and link) it produced. To have any meeting record removed, write to engmohamedkadrialy@gmail.com; removal covers the live database and its backups. Disconnecting an account removes its token from the server immediately.

7. Other participants

People in a meeting see the notetaker in the participant list. The host decides whether to admit it. Anyone who does not want to be recorded can ask the host not to admit it, or ask the operator to delete the record afterwards.

8. Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date.
