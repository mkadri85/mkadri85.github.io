About

Two disciplines, one engineer

I have spent 17 years running and transforming mission-critical telecom operations: regional NOC command centers, service assurance, event correlation, and large migrations for operators across the Middle East and Africa. My work is measured in uptime, OPEX, and the speed at which a network heals itself.

In parallel I design and build software. I ship production systems on Google Cloud, integrate modern AI models into real workflows, and wire up the data and messaging plumbing behind them. The two halves reinforce each other: operations taught me to build things that stay up, and engineering lets me automate the operations.

The result is a rare profile - someone who can own a problem from the org chart down to the deploy pipeline: define the transformation, architect the system, write the code, and run it in production.
