About

Two disciplines, one engineer

My first 13 years were carrier-grade microwave and transmission delivery and operations: planning and integrating multi-vendor links (Huawei, Ericsson, NEC and others), clearing transmission networks of alarms, and negotiating SLA-based acceptance and billing with operators. I rose from site engineer to leading delivery and network-operations teams across four countries.

At Huawei I moved into NOC digital transformation: intelligent assurance, ML-driven event management, and OSS cloud migration on the long-running du mobile-broadband program, delivered alongside executive ROI business cases for C-level stakeholders.

In parallel I design and build software. I own a live mobile and web product end to end - a Flutter app on the iOS and Android stores and a Next.js and Node.js platform - built MCP-based AI automation for campaign orchestration, and run it all on Google Cloud with CI/CD across GitHub and GitLab. The result is a rare profile: someone who can own a problem from the org chart down to the deploy pipeline.
