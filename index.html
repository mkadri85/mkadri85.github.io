About

Two disciplines, one engineer

For 17 years I have delivered and transformed mission-critical networks for operators across the Middle East and Africa, rising from hands-on engineering to leading multi-vendor teams, network operations, and full digital-transformation programs at Huawei and Etisalat. I am used to owning delivery against hard SLAs, managing customer and executive stakeholders, and turning technical decisions into ROI the business can see.

In parallel I design and build software. I own a live consumer product end to end: a Flutter app on the iOS and Android stores and a Next.js and Node.js web platform. I built MCP-based AI automation for campaign orchestration, integrate Claude, Gemini and OpenAI into real workflows, and run the whole thing on Google Cloud with CI/CD across GitHub and GitLab. I am currently building big-data platforms with AI built in.

The result is a rare profile: someone who can own a problem from the org chart down to the deploy pipeline - set the strategy, manage the stakeholders, architect the system, write the code, and run it in production.
